Glowbook Uploader

Glowbook Uploader is a private, single-user tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel Glowbook Bible Stories (channel ID UCGezXqYJPkWo0uqMEmDK8cQ) to upload and schedule the channel's own original videos.

  • It runs on the owner's private home server. It has no other users and no public sign-up.
  • It only uploads the owner's own content (animated Bible stories for kids made for this channel) to the owner's own channel.
  • All videos are declared made for kids and as containing synthetic media.
  • It does not read, collect or store data about viewers or other channels.
